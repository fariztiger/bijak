# BIJAK-WEB-001 — Customer Journey & Conversion Strategy

**Mission:** BIJAK Digital Experience & Product Discovery Platform · **Date:** 2026-11-25

## 1. The six-stage visitor journey

The entire site is organised around one path. Each section maps to exactly one stage, so a visitor always knows where they are and what to do next.

| Stage | Section(s) | Visitor question answered | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Hook** | Hero, Challenges | "What is this, and does it speak to my problem?" | Try a Free Activity · Download Sample |
| **2. Connection** | Method, tone, mascot | "Do I trust these people to teach my child / pupil?" | Discover the 4M Method |
| **3. Discovery** | Method cards, Research timeline, Products | "How does it work, and what exactly do I get?" | Explore BIJAK Products |
| **4. Experience** | 4M Lab, Sample Viewer | "Can I see it working before I buy?" | Try a Free Learning Activity |
| **5. Trust** | Research honesty, specs, comparison, QA narrative | "Is the math real and the quality honest?" | Download a Free Sample |
| **6. Conversion** | Audiences, CTAs, Payments, Enquiry | "What is my next step?" | Get Your BIJAK Workbook · Contact |

## 2. Stage-by-stage strategy

### Stage 1 — Hook
The hero must earn attention in under three seconds. The headline states the value proposition in plain language ("Kukuhkan pemahaman, bukan hanya hafalan" / "Strengthen understanding, not just memorisation") and immediately offers the two lowest-risk actions: **a free interactive activity** and **a free sample**. No price, no commitment — the barrier to the first meaningful interaction is zero.

The four challenge cards reframe real educational problems (vague concepts, memorisation over understanding, low confidence, unstructured practice) as the *reason the product exists*. These are genuine pedagogical problems, presented without fear-mongering — no "your child will fail" messaging.

### Stage 2 — Connection
Trust is built by *how* the site speaks, not just what it says: warm illustrations and a friendly mascot paired with a professional editorial layout and plain, factual language. The mascot TIGER BIJAK is the consistent human-friendly guide who appears again in the lab, creating a coherent personality across the journey.

### Stage 3 — Discovery
The Method section reduces the 4M framework to four scannable cards. The Research timeline then tells the full development story (research → framework → module development → math verification → publishing engine → QA → final product) in accessible language. This is where technical work is translated into customer value: visitors see *why* the product is structured the way it is. The honesty box explicitly separates educational inspiration, implemented methodology, verified quality, and future research objectives — a trust signal in itself.

### Stage 4 — Experience
The 4M Lab is the conversion engine's heart: visitors **do** the methodology rather than read about it. M1–M3 are short interactive quizzes with immediate feedback and mascot coaching; M4 adds a mastery problem plus a single-solution times-table maze that mirrors the actual workbook activity. The Sample Viewer lets visitors flip through real, deliberately selected pages. Both let a prospect experience quality before purchase.

### Stage 5 — Trust
Trust is earned through verifiable specifics: real page counts, real editions, real sample pages, real answer-book separation, real maze construction. The comparison table shows the two editions honestly (identical content, different print economy). The research-honesty and payment-boundary notes demonstrate that the company does not inflate claims.

### Stage 6 — Conversion
Every audience gets a specific, low-friction next step. The five primary CTAs (free activity, free sample, explore products, get workbook, contact) are all reachable from multiple points in the journey; the five secondary CTAs (methodology, research, school packages, distribution, collaboration) route to the enquiry form with a clear role/interest context. There is **no simulated purchase** — the enquiry form is the honest conversion point until live payment is approved.

Each pathway is surfaced in the Audiences section and reinforced by matching CTA buttons throughout the site.

## 4. Conversion mechanics

1. **Zero-friction entry.** The two primary actions (free activity, free sample) require no account, no email, no payment. This maximises first-engagement.
2. **Progressive commitment.** The journey escalates commitment gradually: free activity → free sample → product exploration → enquiry. No stage asks for more than the visitor's comfort level.
3. **Immediate feedback loop.** The lab's live score and feedback give instant satisfaction and demonstrate the pedagogy in action — the most persuasive "pitch" is a correct answer with a mascot cheer.
4. **Honest friction.** Because live payment is not active, the enquiry form is the single conversion endpoint. It asks for minimal data (name, email, role, interest, optional message) with a visible privacy note. The confirmation message is honest: the enquiry is received, a human will respond — no fake payment receipt.
5. **Repeatable hooks.** Sample download and the lab can be revisited freely, encouraging return visits and word-of-mouth sharing of the free experience.

## 5. Malaysian market — payment & digital delivery plan

This is a **plan, not an active integration** (mission boundary: no live payments without founder authorization). The mechanisms proposed for rollout:

- **Payments:** Dual Signal (Malaysian card processor), FPX (direct bank transfer for major Malaysian banks), bank transfer, and major e-wallets (GrabPay, Touch n Go eWallet, Boost) via a licensed payment gateway.
- **Digital delivery:** controlled download links with one-time access keys, or STRATA ContentVault integration giving registered users controlled access to purchased materials.
- **Physical delivery:** registered post / courier for printed workbooks, with tracking after order confirmation.
- **No invented figures:** no prices, sales figures, or payment confirmations appear anywhere on the site until approved and integrated.

## 6. Measurement (to be implemented post-approval)

Once deployed with approval, conversion should be measured on:
- Sample download rate (free-sample CTA).
- Lab engagement (stage completion, maze solve rate).
- Enquiry form completion rate by role/interest.
- Scroll depth through the research timeline (Discovery engagement).
- (No children's data is collected; analytics must remain privacy-compliant.)

## 7. What is explicitly NOT done

- No fear-based marketing, no unrealistic promises, no guarantees of academic improvement.
- No invented prices, sales figures, testimonials, or payment confirmations.
- No presentation of unfinished Volumes 02–05 as available.
- No collection of children's personal data.
